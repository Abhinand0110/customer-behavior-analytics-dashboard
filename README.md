🛍️ Customer Shopping Behavior Analysis

📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes customer shopping behavior using Python, SQL, and Power BI to uncover purchasing patterns, identify revenue drivers, evaluate customer segments, and generate actionable business insights.

The project follows a complete end-to-end data analytics workflow, starting from data preparation and business analysis to dashboard creation and insight generation.

🎯 Objectives

The main objectives of this project were:

Analyze customer purchasing behavior
Identify key revenue-generating categories
Understand customer segmentation
Evaluate discount usage trends
Analyze subscription behavior
Create an interactive dashboard for business decision-making

🔍 Key Insights

Revenue Insights
Clothing generates the highest revenue among all product categories.
High-value customers contribute the largest share of total revenue.
Adult customers are the primary revenue-generating age group.
Customer Insights
Non-subscribers contribute a significant portion of overall revenue.
High-value and medium-value customers drive most business performance.
Approximately 43% of customers use discounts.
Purchase Behavior Insights
Revenue is concentrated within a few high-performing customer segments.
Customer value segmentation significantly impacts purchasing behavior.
Different age groups exhibit different spending patterns.

💡 Business Recommendations

1. Focus on High-Value Customers

Increase marketing efforts toward customers with high purchase value.

2. Improve Subscription Adoption

Encourage non-subscribers to join loyalty or subscription programs.

3. Prioritize High-Performing Categories

Allocate inventory and marketing budgets toward categories such as Clothing and Accessories.

4. Personalized Discount Strategies

Offer targeted discounts based on customer behavior and purchase history.

5. Customer Segmentation

Use customer segmentation for more effective marketing campaigns.
